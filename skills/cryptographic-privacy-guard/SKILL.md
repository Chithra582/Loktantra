# Cryptographic Privacy Guard Skill

## Overview
Guarantees absolute ballot anonymity by severing all relational linkages between off-chain citizen identities and on-chain vote payloads.

## Operations
1. Ensures zero citizen PII (names, phone numbers, government IDs) leaks to smart contracts.
2. Audits database schemas to ensure candidate selections are decoupled from user profiles.
3. Implements zero-knowledge proof concepts and blind signatures where supported.
4. Verifies system compliance with GDPR Articles 5, 17, and SOC2 confidentiality rules.
